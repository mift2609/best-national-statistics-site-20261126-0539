How to read this site

Every figure carries its source and the year it describes, printed right beside it. Most come from BPS — Statistics Indonesia, the national statistics office — and the rest from the bodies named: the World Bank, the Geospatial Information Agency, the volcanology agency PVMBG and the internet association APJII. Figures are rounded the way their sources publish them. Where two pages could show the same number, they show the same number: the dataset behind this site is a single file, so nothing can drift out of agreement with itself.
