Provinces: 38 and counting

Indonesia began with a handful of provinces and has divided them ever since — most recently in 2022, when Papua became five provinces. West Java alone holds more people than the twenty smallest provinces combined.

Three provinces worth a second look

Jakarta is a province as well as the capital city: about 10.6 million people in just over 660 km², the densest province by far. Yogyakarta is a special region ruled in part by its sultan, who serves as governor by hereditary right. Southwest Papua, created in 2022 with its capital at Sorong, is the newest of the 38 — the fourth new province carved from the old Papua province that year.
