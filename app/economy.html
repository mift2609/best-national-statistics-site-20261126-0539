What the economy makes

The story the series tell

Switch the chart above to GDP growth and the pandemic is unmistakable: −2.07% in 2020, the only contraction in the series, then a return to the five-percent path Indonesia has held for a decade. The poverty series shows the same shock in slower motion — a rise to 10.14% by March 2021, then a steady fall to record lows. Inflation spiked to 5.51% in 2022 as fuel prices were adjusted, and by December 2024 it was 1.57%, the gentlest in the series.
