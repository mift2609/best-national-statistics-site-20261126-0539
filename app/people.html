People: 281.6 million, unevenly spread

Indonesia is the world's fourth most populous country — but more than half of everyone lives on one island, Java, which holds less than a tenth of the land. This page is about where people are, and what they are like in aggregate.

Where people live

The 2020 census counted 270.2 million people. Java's 151.6 million make it one of the most populous islands on Earth; the two eastern island groups together hold fewer people than the single province of East Java.

Reading the profile

Three numbers explain much of the rest of this site. A fertility rate of about 2.1 means the population is still growing — people live longer and past generations were large — but the growth the Overview chart shows is bending: BPS projects the count to peak later this century. A median age of 30.7 makes Indonesia young beside Japan or Europe, which is why jobs and schooling dominate its politics. And a 58.6% urban share, climbing steadily, is why the Provinces page is topped by the great Javanese provinces and their cities.
